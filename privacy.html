Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Draft for review. This is a starting-point policy. Please review it (ideally with a professional) and confirm it reflects your actual practices before publishing.

Curated Travel by Crystal ("we," "us," or "I") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. Travel is booked through our host agency, Fora Travel, and some information is shared with travel suppliers as needed to arrange your trip.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through the inquiry form: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), trip type, budget range, destinations, travel dates, your message, and whether you opt in to our newsletter.
  • Information needed to book travel, which you provide directly when you become a client (for example traveler names, dates of birth, and travel documents).
  • Basic technical data that web hosts and form services log automatically, such as IP address and browser type.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide travel-planning services.
  • To research, recommend, book, and support your travel arrangements.
  • To send you newsletters or offers, if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To meet legal, accounting, and supplier requirements.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide our services, including with:

  • Fora Travel, our host agency, which supports bookings and payments.
  • Travel suppliers (hotels, resorts, cruise lines, airlines, tour operators, insurers) to make and manage your reservations.
  • Service providers that help operate this site, such as our form provider (Formspree) and email tools.
  • Authorities when required by law.

Newsletter & marketing

If you opt in, we may email you travel inspiration, guides, and offers. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time by contacting us.

Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices & rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing emails. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond promptly.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and we use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
